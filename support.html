Contact

Email hello@tactician.run. Include the email address you sign in with and, for a sync problem, the date of the activity. A person reads every message.

Tactician is in pre-launch. If you are not yet using the app, join the waitlist.

Getting Started

Sign in with Apple or Google, set your goal race, and connect Strava. Tactician needs Strava: your activities are its only input, so without them it cannot compute load or update the day's directive.

Strava Sync

Activities sync when you open the app and in the background. If a run is missing, open the app to trigger a sync, and check the activity is visible to Tactician in Strava. If Settings shows Credentials lost · sync halted, reconnect Strava from Settings.

To disconnect Tactician from Strava, remove it in your Strava settings.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple.

  • Cancel or change plan: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions
  • Restore a purchase on a new device: tap Restore Purchase on the subscription screen
  • Refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com

Full terms, including automatic renewal and the free trial, are in the Terms of Use.

Delete Your Account

In the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This removes your account, training plan and workout history, and disconnects Strava. It cannot be undone. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that separately. You can also ask for deletion by email at hello@tactician.run.
