Privacy Notice

Last updated · October 2026
Who We Are

Tactician runs tactician.run and the Tactician iOS app. This notice covers both: the site and its waitlist, and the app once it is available. Questions about your data go to hello@tactician.run.

The Site
Waitlist

When you join the waitlist we collect your name and email address, and optionally your goal race and experience level. If you arrived from a tagged link, we also store which campaign it was (UTM source, medium, campaign, content). We use this to run the waitlist and to email you about Tactician's launch and early access. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Analytics and Session Recording

Vercel Web Analytics counts page views. It sets no cookies and does not identify you across sites.

Microsoft Clarity runs only if you accept it in the banner shown on your first visit. If you accept, Clarity records how you use the site (pages viewed, scrolling, clicks and mouse movement, with your device type, browser and approximate location) and sets two first-party cookies, _clck and _clsk, to link the pages of one visit. Anything typed into the waitlist form is masked in your browser and never sent to Clarity. If you decline, or your browser sends Global Privacy Control, Clarity does not load at all. You can change your choice at any time from the Cookies link in the site footer.

The App
Your Account

You sign in with Apple or Google. We receive your name, email address and an account identifier from the provider you choose. If you use Sign in with Apple and hide your email, we receive Apple's relay address instead.

Strava Data

Tactician works from your Strava training history, and reads it only after you connect Strava and authorise access. From each activity we read the sport type, date, distance, duration, pace, elevation, heart rate, cadence, power, laps and streams. Tactician does not read health data from other sources: no HRV, no sleep, no Apple Health.

Your Strava access token is stored in your device's Keychain. The Strava app secret never ships in the app; token exchanges run through our server. You can disconnect Tactician from Strava at any time in your Strava settings.

Training Data You Enter

Your race target and goal time, any recent race result you state, your training plan, and every change you or the engine make to it. This is stored against your account so it syncs across your devices, and row-level security limits each record to its owner.

Subscriptions

Purchases are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an app user identifier so the app can confirm whether your subscription is active.

App Analytics and Notifications

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to record how the app is used (screens and actions, device type, app version). It is not used for advertising, and Tactician does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Reminders and notifications are scheduled on your device.

Both
Who Processes It

We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising. These providers process it on our behalf, each for the purpose named:

  • Loops: waitlist and launch email
  • Vercel: site hosting and page-view counts
  • Microsoft Clarity: session recording, only with your consent. Microsoft acts as an independent controller under the Microsoft Privacy Statement
  • Supabase: app database and sign-in
  • Strava: the source of your activity data
  • Google: Google sign-in and Firebase app analytics
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, payments and notifications
  • RevenueCat: subscription status

Several of these providers are based in the United States, so your data may be processed there under the providers' contractual safeguards for international transfers.

Legal Basis

For visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: we process app data to provide the service you signed up for (contract); waitlist email, Clarity and the Strava connection rest on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time; cookieless page counts and keeping the service secure rest on our legitimate interest.

Retention and Deletion

Waitlist: kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We remove a deletion request within 30 days.

Clarity: recordings are deleted after 30 days. Recordings we save, and a random sample Clarity keeps, last up to 9 months. Clarity cannot delete a single visitor's recordings on request, so they expire on this schedule.

App account: kept while your account exists. Delete it in the app under Settings → Delete Account, or by email. Deletion removes your account, training plan, workout history and stated results, and disconnects Strava. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; cancel that in your Apple ID settings. Apple and RevenueCat keep purchase records as the law requires.

Children

Tactician is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and the waitlist is for people 16 or older. Google, Apple and Strava accounts carry their own minimum ages. If you believe a child has given us their information, email us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act, you can ask to access, correct, delete or export the personal data we hold about you, and withdraw any consent you have given. Email hello@tactician.run; we respond within 30 days. You will not be treated differently for exercising these rights. In the EU you can also complain to your data protection authority; in Portugal that is the CNPD.

Changes

When this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it. If a change affects how we use data we already hold, we will tell you before it takes effect.
