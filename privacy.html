Privacy Notice

Last updated · October 2026
What We Collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect your name, email address, and optionally your goal race and experience level. If you arrived from a tagged link, we also store which campaign it was (UTM source, medium, campaign, content).

When you browse the site, Microsoft Clarity records how you use it: pages viewed, scrolling, clicks, and mouse movement, along with your device type, browser, and approximate location. Anything you type into the waitlist form is masked in your browser and never sent to Clarity.

Why

To manage the waitlist and contact you about Tactician's launch. We will not email you for any other purpose. Clarity data shows us how visitors use the site, so we can improve it.

Who Sees It

Your data is processed by Loops (our email service provider, US-based) and stored on servers provided by Vercel (our hosting provider). No one else has access to your waitlist details. We do not sell your personal information.

Site usage data is collected by Microsoft Clarity. Microsoft operates Clarity as an independent controller and handles that data under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

There are no advertising pixels on this site. We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views; it is cookieless and does not identify you across sites. Clarity sets two first-party cookies (_clck and _clsk) to link the pages you view in one visit. For visitors in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, Clarity does not set these cookies.

Legal Basis

We rely on legitimate interest and your consent at the point of signup. Submitting the form is your affirmative action. You can withdraw at any time.

Clarity usage data is collected based on our legitimate interest in understanding how the site is used and improving it.

Retention

We hold your data until Tactician launches or until you unsubscribe, whichever comes first. If you request deletion, we will remove your record within 30 days.

Clarity recordings are deleted after 30 days. Recordings we save, and a random sample Clarity keeps, last up to 9 months. Clarity cannot delete a single visitor's recordings on request, so they expire on this schedule.

Your Rights

Under GDPR and applicable law, you have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@tactician.run. We will respond within 30 days.

To opt out of Clarity, turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser, use the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out at optout.aboutads.info (select Microsoft), or block clarity.ms with a content blocker. The site works the same either way.
