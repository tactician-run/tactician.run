Problem · 01

Static training plans break when life happens.

Every plan assumes perfect adherence, perfect recovery, perfect weeks. The first time you miss a session, or push too hard, the rest of the block is wrong, and you're guessing.

  • 01 Missed workouts cascade into broken weeks. Adherence
  • 02 Fatigue accumulates silently. The ceiling stays hidden. Load
  • 03 Recovery is inconsistent, but the plan isn't. Rigidity
  • 04 No rule to decide the days that matter. Judgement
  • 05 Race day arrives, and nothing has told you what the block actually supports. Projection

System · 02

A rules-based engine. Five rules. One directive per day.

Overload, intensity stacking, recovery, undertraining, ramp. Five rules, evaluated in priority order on every synced session. Deterministic. Auditable. The engine proposes; you decide what reaches the plan.

01 · Engine

Daily Decision Output

Today's session reflects what actually happened. Tactician reads completed workouts, checks them against the rules, and proposes one change to the prescription. Nothing moves until you slide to apply it.
